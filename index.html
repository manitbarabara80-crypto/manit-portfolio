HELLO, I'M MANIT

A future-focused developer with a purpose beyond code.

I’m Manit Barabara Tebligu, a fourth-year Computer Science and Engineering student at Bule Hora University, Ethiopia, working toward my graduation in 2028. I’m building my path in software engineering through hands-on projects, continuous learning and a strong interest in solving practical problems with technology.

My work and learning focus on web development and full-stack fundamentals. I use HTML, CSS and JavaScript to create front-end experiences, and I’m developing my back-end skills with PHP and MySQL. I’m also exploring Android app development, including building mobile applications with Java and Android tools. I work with C++ and Python as I strengthen my programming and problem-solving foundation.

My connection to South Omo and its pastoralist communities shapes the kind of impact I hope to make. It reminds me that talent exists everywhere, while access to technology and opportunity is not always equal. I want to help build useful digital tools, share local stories with wider audiences, and contribute to solutions that respond to real needs.

I’m open to learning from experienced developers, contributing to meaningful projects, and connecting with mentors, teams and organizations that value curiosity, consistency and community-centered innovation.
